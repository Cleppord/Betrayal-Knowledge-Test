The Knowledge Test

Do you have the wits to survive the house?

20 multiple-choice questions: 10 quick multiplication problems, then 10 electrical and computer engineering questions.

You have 3 minutes total. Unanswered questions count as wrong.

Your Knowledge trait is graded ruthlessly. Accuracy sets your starting score: 20/20 is an 8, one wrong is a 6, and every further mistake drops you lower. Then the clock bites: you get a 30-second grace period, and after that you lose 1 point for every 15 seconds (minimum trait of 1).
